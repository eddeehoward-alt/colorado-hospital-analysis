# Key Findings

## Dataset Overview

- The CMS dataset contains 5,432 hospital records nationwide.
- Colorado has 97 hospital records.

## Emergency Services

- 88 Colorado hospitals offer emergency services.
- 9 Colorado hospitals do not offer emergency services.
- 90.7% of Colorado hospitals offer emergency services.

## Hospital Types

- Acute Care Hospitals: 51
- Critical Access Hospitals: 32
- Psychiatric Hospitals: 9
- Children's Hospitals: 2
- Veterans Administration Acute Care Hospitals: 2
- Department of Defense Acute Care Hospitals: 1

## Ownership

- Nonprofit hospitals: 52
- Government hospitals: 32
- For-profit hospitals: 13

## Hospital Ratings

- 49 Colorado hospitals have an available CMS overall rating.
- 48 Colorado hospitals do not have an available rating.
- Rating availability is 50.5%.
- The average rating among hospitals with available ratings is 3.96 stars.
- Colorado has 14 five-star hospitals.

## Geographic Distribution

- 68 Colorado cities are represented in the dataset.
- 53 cities have exactly one hospital.
- 77.9% of represented cities have only one hospital.

## Important Limitation

Hospital counts do not measure true access. This analysis does not include population, hospital capacity, distance, travel time, or service-area size.
